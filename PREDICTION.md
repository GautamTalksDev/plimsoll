## Prediction

I predict **KT-1 will FAIL**. I do not expect 25 distinct organisations to have
published a seal by 21 October 2026. With no distribution, one maintainer, and
a mechanism that asks researchers to publish something they are not currently
asked for, I would be surprised by more than five.

I predict **KT-2 will remain NOT EVALUABLE**, because it requires N >= 100
sealed evaluations and KT-1 failing implies nowhere near that volume.

I am less certain about **KT-3**. A single inbound request for a private log or
evidence export is a much lower bar than 25 publishing organisations, and the
buyer for it is not the same person as the user.

## Why this file exists

The bar of 25 organisations was set in the first commit, before I knew anything
about how the outreach would land. In week seven it will feel reasonable to
argue that the number was arbitrary, chosen without data, and therefore not
binding. That argument will be available to me whether or not it is true.

This file records that I believed the bar was fair on the day I predicted I
would fail it. If I later want to move it, I have to explain what changed other
than my knowing the answer.

The threshold does not move. If it fails, it gets published.
